TindmxClips

TindmxClips is a YouTube Shorts channel that posts highlights from livestreams on Twitch, Kick and YouTube. Every clip is posted with the streamer's permission, credits the streamer by name, and links back to their channel.

This site also documents clipbot, the private tool that edits these highlights into Shorts and uploads them to the TindmxClips YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. clipbot is used only by the channel owner and only uploads to the channel owner's own YouTube account.

Streamers

If you're a streamer and want your clips removed, or want to be featured, email tindmxclips@gmail.com. Removal requests are honored promptly.
