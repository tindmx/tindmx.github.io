Lab Rat Logic HQ

Lab Rat Logic HQ is an evidence-first science channel on YouTube (@LabRatLogicHQ) and TikTok. Each week it publishes one explainer about the research behind popular peptides and medicines (what was studied, in animals or in people, and how strong the evidence is), plus a few Shorts cut from that episode. Every episode lists its sources. Nothing on the channel is medical advice.

clipbot

This site also documents clipbot, the private publishing tool behind the channel. clipbot assembles each episode (narration, diagrams, licensed stock footage) and, after the channel owner approves it, uploads the episode, its thumbnail and its Shorts to the channel's own YouTube account using the YouTube Data API, and can post the Shorts to the channel's own TikTok account. clipbot is operated only by the channel owner and only publishes to the owner's own accounts; it is not offered to the public.
